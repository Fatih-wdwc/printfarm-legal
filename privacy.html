Privacy Policy

Print Farm Tycoon · Last updated: 4 October 2026

Short version: the game works without an account and never asks for your name, email, contacts or location. If you use cloud save, your game progress is stored on our server under an anonymous ID. Purchases are handled by Google Play or the App Store. You can delete your data at any time from Settings › Save & data › Delete my data.

What we collect and why

  • Game progress on your device. Your farm, coins, settings and similar game data are saved on your phone so you can continue playing. This data stays on your device unless you use cloud save.
  • Cloud save (optional). To let your progress move to a new phone, the game creates a random anonymous account ID and a secret key on our server and stores a copy of your game progress there. We do not link it to your name or email. Our server also keeps short-lived technical logs (such as IP address and request time) for security and to fix problems; these are deleted after 30 days.
  • Purchases. In-app purchases are processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We receive a confirmation that a purchase happened and what was bought, so we can give you the item. We never see or store your payment details.
  • Rewarded ads. Some optional rewards can be earned by choosing to watch an ad. Ads are provided by Unity LevelPlay and the ad networks it works with. To show and measure ads, these partners may collect technical data such as a device advertising identifier, device model and approximate location derived from the IP address. Ads are only personalized if you agree to it; otherwise you see non-personalized ads. You can change this choice at any time in Settings › Personalized ads. On iPhone, the system will also ask your permission before any tracking.
  • Usage stats (optional). If you switch on Anonymous usage stats (when you first start the game, or in Settings › Usage stats), the game sends gameplay events to Unity Analytics: for example which tutorial step you reached, when you unlocked a room, and how long a session lasted, together with a random installation ID, device model, operating system, language and country (derived from the IP address). We use this only to balance and improve the game. It is off unless you turn it on, and you can turn it off at any time. The game also downloads balancing settings (Unity Remote Config); this sends only the installation ID and the app version.
  • Notifications. Reminders (such as "your gem chest is ready") are scheduled on your phone. No data is sent to us to do this, and you can turn them off in Settings or in your phone's settings.

We use this data only to run the game, keep your progress, deliver purchases, show the ads you choose to watch, and keep the service secure. We do not sell your data.

Legal bases (EU/UK users)

We process data to provide the game you asked for (performance of a contract), to keep the service secure (legitimate interests), and, for personalized ads and usage stats, only with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

Who we share data with

  • Google (Google Play) and Apple (App Store), for purchases.
  • Unity Technologies (LevelPlay) and its ad network partners, for rewarded ads, under the choices described above.
  • Unity Technologies (Unity Analytics and Remote Config), for the optional usage stats and for balancing settings.
  • Our hosting provider, OVHcloud, which runs the cloud save server for us in a data centre in Poland (EU).

We may also disclose data if required by law.

How long we keep it

Cloud save data is kept while you use the game and deleted when you ask us to delete it, or after 24 months without any activity. Server logs are deleted after 30 days.

Children

Print Farm Tycoon is a game for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Where local law requires it, players under the age of digital consent only see non-personalized ads. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, or object to its use. The fastest way to delete everything is Settings › Save & data › Delete my data in the game, which removes your cloud save and resets the game on your phone. You can also email support@krmnstudios.com. If you are in the EU or UK, you can complain to your local data protection authority; in Denmark that is Datatilsynet (datatilsynet.dk).

Security

Data sent between the game and our server is encrypted (HTTPS). Cloud saves can only be read with the secret key stored on your device. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the game.
